## TIER DEFINITIONS

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| S+ | Best of the best. Irreplaceable. Defines the meta. |
| S | Excellent. Core to top-tier formations. |
| A+ | Very good. Competitive in most scenarios. Worth full investment. |
| A | Good. Solid in role. Worth investment with context. |
| B | Situational. Useful as filler or in specific formations. Minimal medals. |
| C | Limited use. Gathering or early-game only. No universal medals. |
| D | Avoid investment entirely. |

## AIGA INVESTMENT RULES (from tier analysis)

- **S+ and S heroes:** Invest fully — universal medals, skill points, XP all justified
- **A+ heroes:** Invest with priority after S-tier is capped
- **A heroes:** Invest contextually — if they're in your active march
- **B heroes:** Minimum investment — 1-3 stars max, no universal medals
- **C heroes:** No universal medals ever — only tavern/event-specific medals
- **D heroes:** Zero investment
