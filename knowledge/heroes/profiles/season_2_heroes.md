**Rarity:** Legendary | **Type:** Cavalry | **Availability:** S2 Advent Wheel
**Specialties:** [verify in-game — Drive source link found but file not in collection]
**Tier:** S+ | **Role:** W.CAV Lead — dominant S2 formation
