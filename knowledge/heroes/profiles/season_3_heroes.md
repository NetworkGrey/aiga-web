**Rarity:** Legendary | **Type:** Pikemen | **Availability:** S3 Advent Wheel
**Specialties:** Warrior / Support / Buff
**Tier:** A | **Role:** W.PIK Support / PIK 3rd slot

**Base Stats (lv1):** Might 84.2 (+1.02) | Armor 71.03 (+0.80) | Strategy 75.69 (+0.83) | Siege 47.45 (+0.65)

**Skills:**
| # | Name | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Spend a Fortune | Commander | Might to 2 troops (163.90%) + enemy commander armor -20.86 for 6s. 100 initial rage. |
| 2 | Devout Radiance | Signature | After troop takes 12 normal attacks: might (119.20%) + troop damage taken -3.58% for 3s. |
| 3 | Vigilance | Secondary | 15% chance to counterattack (18.95%) + 50% rout chance (7.45%/s for 3s). |
| 4 | Mettle | Secondary | After 12 normal attacks taken: recovers units (37.91% might + armor). |

| 7 | Tenacity | Passive | Recovers units every 9s (65.65% armor). |
| 8 | King's Blade | Secondary | Might every 9s (59.60%) + 46% chance all hero damage +6.06% for 3s. |

**AIGA Notes:** Replaces Barbarossa in W.PIK march when S3 arrives. "Manza keeps everyone alive." Critical part of S4 Cyrus combo. Signature provides consistent damage reduction on a trigger-count basis.
