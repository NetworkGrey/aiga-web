### HUA MULAN
**Rarity:** Legendary | **Type:** Archer | **Availability:** S1 Advent Wheel
**Specialties:** Warrior / Attack / Secondary Strike
**Tier:** A+ (S in W.ARC context) | **Role:** W.ARC Lead (Spender)

**Base Stats (lv1):** Might 96.98 (+0.96) | Armor 65.50 (+0.64) | Strategy 74.04 (+0.68) | Siege 45.78 (+0.43)

**Skills:**
| # | Name | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Flying Swallow | Commander | 4x might damage to target (43.21% each). Grants 150 initial rage. |
| 2 | Starchaser | Signature | Might damage (83.44%); 50% chance to split into 3 instances (41.72% each). |
| 3 | Armor Piercer | Secondary | Might (55.75%) + reduces all enemy armor by 14.90 for 3s. |
| 4 | Infuriation | Secondary | Might (55.75%) + increases own might by 14.90 for 3s. |
| 5 | Act of Mercy | Secondary | Might (47.47%) + increases damage by 2% per 1% enemy units lost (max 100%). |
| 6 | Double Attack | Secondary | Double attack state + might/strategy/armor +4.04 for 3s. |
| 7 | Furious Charge | Passive | Each sig activation increases might damage by 3.94%, up to 5 stacks. |
| 8 | Weak Spot Attack | Secondary | Might (74.74%) + increases own damage by 6.06% for 3s. |

**Recommended Build:** Flying Swallow / Starchaser / Weak Spot Attack / Double Attack
**Best Pairings:** Attila the Hun, Bellevue, Rani Durgavati, Mehmed II (S3), King Derek
**AIGA Notes:** Best S1 ARC lead. Starchaser multi-hit synergises with Attila double attack. Mehmed replaces Attila at S3. Carries well into S3+.

### SEJONG THE GREAT
**Rarity:** Legendary | **Type:** Cavalry | **Availability:** Tavern
**Specialties:** Tactician / Defense / Recovery
**Tier:** B | **Role:** M.CAV 2IC (tankiest hero in game) / Gathering support

**Key Skill:** Reduces hero's own damage dealt but reduces troops' damage taken. Peaceful Haven + Tenacity + Protracted Battle for maximum tankiness. 50% chance to recover units on skill damage.

**AIGA Notes:** "One of the tankiest goddamn heroes in the game." Functions as a near-unkillable support in M.CAV. Some players try Sejong in rally for damage reduction. Do not invest universal medals — earn through tavern over time.

### KING ARTHUR

**Specialties:** [verify in-game]
