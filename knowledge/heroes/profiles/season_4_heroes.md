## S4 ADVENT WHEEL

---

### LAGERTHA
**Rarity:** Legendary | **Type:** Swordsmen | **Availability:** S4 Advent Wheel
**Specialties:** Warrior / Attack / Active
**Tier:** S | **Role:** W.SW 3rd Slot (endgame)

**Key Mechanic — Valkyrie's Edge / Judgment:**
Lagertha's signature skill has a **65% chance to make the commander activate their signature skill a second time.** This double-cast is her core value.

Additional: Piercing damage ignores 20% of enemy defense (defense-ignoring damage).

**How it compounds with Miyamoto:**
- Miyamoto fires Moment of Yin (390% damage)
- Lagertha's Judgment: 65% chance Miyamoto fires again
- Both instances have a 45% chance to trigger Lagertha's own damage boost (+30%)
- "Double-casting your main skills because of the Judgment proc"

**Best Pairings:** Miyamoto Musashi, King Arthur, Yodit, Tribhuwana, Cypio (S6)
**AIGA Notes:** S tier — not S+, but "better than Tribhuwana and on same paper as Miyamoto." Locks in W.SW endgame. Gets dramatically more powerful when Cypio (S6) arrives — Cypio makes Lagertha's piercing damage crit.
**Drive source:** Not in collection — verify in-game.

### ELIZABETH I
**Rarity:** Legendary | **Type:** Pikemen | **Availability:** S4 Events
**Specialties:** [verify in-game]
**Tier:** S+ | **Role:** T.PIK Support (irreplaceable)

**Key Mechanic — Assimilation (life steal):**
Converts 10% of damage dealt back into troops recovered. "At 30,000 damage proc = 3,000 troops instantly healed." Compounds with health gems for greater return.

Star progression: 1-star = conversion rate +5%. 3-star = every 2 assimilation effects increases commander damage by 10%. 5-star = damage boost +5%, always calculates against enemy's lower stat.

**Best Pairings:** Belisarius, Cyrus, Ashoka
**AIGA Notes:** "Best support for pikes and there's no one going to come close." Versatile: crushing impact or triple offensive skill options. "She is not going to get replaced." S+ projected indefinitely.
**Drive source:** Not in collection — verify in-game.
