## RINGS OVERVIEW

Rings equip one per hero. Unlock at Town Centre level 18. 35 rings across 3 tiers. Every hero should have a ring — any ring beats no ring.

**Key rule:** Rings return to inventory when replaced. They are never lost on swap.

**Best Tier 1 offence:** Ring of Steed (22% troop damage) or Ring of Shark (22.8% skill damage)
**Best Tier 1 survival:** Ring of Boar (17.6% damage + 15.1% damage reduction)
**Best Tier 1 balanced:** Ring of Crow or Ring of Steed

### Tier 1 skill notes
- **Twist of Fate (Crow):** Steals stats from enemy commander. Synergises with Hannibal who also debuffs enemy stats.
- **Ablaze Spirit (Night Wolf):** Boosts normal attack + secondary strike damage after every 9 attacks. Good for Hannibal, Hua Mulan, Lu Bu secondary-strike marches.
- **Blessing of Oasis (Falcon):** Every 9s — either recovers units OR reduces damage taken. Reliable passive value for support heroes.
- **All Out (Bear):** Reduces own heroes' armor by 20, grants commander might +22.05. High risk — only viable with strong armor gems.
- **Burning Will (Boar):** Strong defensive passive — damage + damage reduction simultaneously.
- **Coercion (Deer):** Stacks up to 50 times for 10.5% recovery reduction on the enemy. Strong in prolonged fights against Justinian or recovery-heavy builds.

| **Everflame Wings** — Silencing Flame | Every 9s: burn DoT (2.37%/s) + strategy damage taken +0.95% for 9s | Synergises with Suleiman (Magnificent Conquest bonus vs burned targets). Good against strategy-heavy formations. |
| **Radiant Guardian** — Flurry of Blows | Secondary strike activation +0.50% every 6s + 40% chance double attack | Best healing ring in game. Best on secondary-strike support heroes. Despite the name, not offensive. |
| **Skyward Knight** — Cost of Victory | Hero deals 15% less damage (cannot purify) + commander damage +3.23% + sig active activation +1.90% | **Penalty is real.** Best on a support hero where personal damage matters less. NOT on a DPS lead. |

| **Lord of Eastern Heavens** — Decree of Victory | Active skill damage -25% (cannot purify) + normal attack damage +19% | Best on normal-attack / counterattack heroes (Boudica, Leonidas, Richard). Harmful on active-skill leads (Sun Tzu, Suleiman). |
| **Messenger of Destruction** — Perception | Normal attack damage -20% (cannot purify) + passive skill damage +14.25% | Best on passive-skill dependent heroes. Harmful on normal-attack builds. |

## RING PRIORITY BY HERO

| Hero | Recommended ring | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Lu Bu** | Ring of Daisy | BIS confirmed 40+ battle reports. Never change. |
| **Hua Mulan** | Ring of Steed or Ring of Crow (T1) → Lord of Eastern Heavens (T2) | Secondary strike — Crow's Twist of Fate pairs well |
| **Miyamoto Musashi** | Ring of Tulip (current lv10) → Ring of Steed or Night Wolf (T1) → Lord of Eastern Heavens (T2) | Lord activates Musashi's normal attack loop |
| **Attila** | Ring of Tulip or Clover (T0) → Ring of Steed (T1) | Slot 3 support — high activation count |
| **Hannibal / Belisarius** | Ring of Crow (T1) — Twist of Fate steals commander stats | Synergises with Hannibal's debuff kit |
| **Theodora** | Ring of Steed or Shark (T1) → Skyward Knight (T2 as support) | Strategy build — sig activation boost from Skyward useful |
| **Justinian / Hammurabi** | Ring of Falcon (T1) → Radiant Guardian (T2) | Healing + recovery role |
| **Octavian / Julius Caesar** | Ring of Steed (T1) → Sacred Sage (T2 if pure turn-based) | Sacred Sage only if march has zero active-skill dependency |
| **Suleiman** | Ring of Shark (T1) → Azure Moon or Everflame Wings (T2) | Burn synergy with Magnificent Conquest |
| **Ramesses II** | Ring of Shark (T1) → Skyward Knight (T2) | Strategy sig-activation build |
| **Diao Chan / gathering heroes** | Ring of Violet or Sunflower | Gathering stats only. Never a combat ring. |

**General upgrade path:** T0 any → T1 best available → T2 when resources allow. M1 hero first, then M2, then M3/M4/M5.
