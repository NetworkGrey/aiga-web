## OVERVIEW

At account creation, players choose a civilization that provides permanent passive stat bonuses and a unique special troop type. Civilization can be changed in-game *[verify cost and conditions in-game]*.

**Key rule:** Match civilization to your primary M1 march troop type. The troop bonus directly affects your most-used formation.

**9 civilizations confirmed** — 8 documented below. Arabs (9th) confirmed by Van but stat bonuses not documented. *[Add Arabs when verified in-game.]*

**At global launch:** 8 playable civilizations | 20+ hero background civilizations. Heroes from non-playable civilizations still exist (e.g. Leonidas = Greek/Byzantine, Musashi = Japanese civ available).

## THE EIGHT DOCUMENTED CIVILIZATIONS

### FRENCH
**Primary troop:** Swordsmen
**Passive bonuses:**
- Swordsmen Defence +2%
- Swordsmen Training Speed +2%
- Food Production Speed +6%

**Special troop:** Throwing Axemen (replaces standard Swordsmen)
- Greater attack range + enhanced damage vs standard swordsmen
- Launch axes to disrupt enemy formations

**Best for:** Swordsmen-primary march players (early stage or non-specialist)

## CIVILIZATION SELECTION GUIDE

| Primary march | Early/F2P choice | Endgame/PvP choice |
|---|---|---|
| Swordsmen M1 | French (+2% def, training speed, food production) | Japanese (+5% defence/attack) |
| Archer M1 | Chinese (+2% def, training speed, wood production) | British (+5% — defensive) |

## LANDMARKS — IMPERIAL CITY REGION

### Imperial City
Server's ultimate prize. Victorious alliance leader appoints a **King**.

- Clash duration: 4 hours
- Win condition: First to occupy for 1 hour, OR longest total occupation time
- Entry requirement: Alliance territory must border the IC
- **Sacred Towers** (4 towers) — each grants +20% attack AND defence for the matching troop type to troops inside the IC

| Tower | Buff |
|---|---|
| Pike Sacred Tower | Pikemen +20% atk + def inside IC |
| Sword Sacred Tower | Swordsmen +20% atk + def inside IC |
| Cavalry Sacred Tower | Cavalry +20% atk + def inside IC |
| Arrow Sacred Tower | Archers +20% atk + def inside IC |

- **Sun Legion** spawns inside during the event — attacks all governors regardless of alliance
- **Coronation:** Winning alliance leader appoints King. If not appointed before timer expires, leader auto-coronated

## TERRITORY — ALLIANCE STRUCTURES

### Structure Types

| Structure | Notes |
|---|---|
| Alliance Stronghold (Main) | Foundation — buildable anywhere accessible; sub-strongholds connect to main |
| Alliance Stronghold (Sub) | Resource region; connection to main required during construction only |
| Alliance Flags | Expand territory; must connect to stronghold or existing flag; resource/IC region flags cost more but have higher durability |
| Alliance Gathering Center | 1 per alliance; gathering speed bonus; cannot be attacked during operation |
| Tower | Controllable after reinforcing; power caps at 10K reinforcement |

### Territory Research Categories

| Category | Effect |
|---|---|
| Politics Building | Alliance Politics Research Speed |
| Science Building | Alliance Science Research Speed |
| Economy Building | Alliance Economy Research Speed |
| Military Building | Alliance Military Research Speed |
| Faith Building | Alliance Faith Research Speed |
| Facility Building | Alliance Facility Research Speed |

Each category has up to 7 levels.

## ALLIANCE WONDER BUILDINGS

Won through Wonder Contest. Permanent alliance structures with own tech trees.

| Wonder | Primary effect |
|---|---|
| Empire Arena | Ally defence +41% on adjacent flags |
| Tower of Babel | Ally attack +10% on adjacent flags |
| Tornado Wonder | Releases tornadoes to Alliance lands for 10 minutes |
| Lightning Wonder | Releases lightning to specific land for 30 minutes |

Effects apply to **adjacent flags** — placement matters. Build and max tech in home kingdom; deploy to strategic map locations during KvK/MEE.
