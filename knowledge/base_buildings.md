## TOWN CENTRE OVERVIEW

TC is the single most important metric. No building can exceed TC level. Rush TC17 as the primary mid-game goal — it unlocks 3 heroes per march.

**Key milestones:**

| TC | What unlocks |
|---|---|
| 2 | Hospital |
| 5 | Join alliance |
| 6 | Market |
| 7 | Dock (fishing) |
| 8 | Embassy |
| 12 | War Hall + 2nd hero per march |
| 15 | Smithy (gear forge) |
| 17 | **3rd hero per march (maximum)** |
| 18 | Rings system |

### Heroes per march by TC level

| TC range | Heroes per march |
|---|---|
| 1-11 | 1 (commander only) |
| 12-16 | 2 |
| 17-30 | **3 (maximum)** |

Note: This is separate from Deployable Troops research (unlocks additional marches). Hero slots control heroes per march; Deployable Troops controls number of simultaneous marches.

## BUILDING UPGRADE PRIORITY

1. **Town Centre** — always the primary target
2. **University** — keep at max level at all times (research speed + unlocks)
3. **Tavern** — hero level cap + free recruitment cooldown
4. **Embassy** — more assists = faster queues; upgrade aggressively
5. **Hospital** — upgrade at every TC milestone requiring it (TC11/16/21/28)
6. **Military Barracks (all 4)** — training speed + troop tier unlocks
7. **Smithy** (unlocks TC15) — upgrade immediately; reduces forge time significantly; up to 5 forge queues at max
8. **City Walls** — upgrade whenever possible (TC prerequisite)
9. **War Hall** (unlocks TC12) — rally capacity + daily pillage limit
10. **Watchtower** — meet TC prerequisites only, nothing more
11. **Market** — meet TC prerequisites only, nothing more
12. **Guard Towers** — last, after all critical buildings are current

**Production buildings (Mills, Quarries, Lumber Camps, Gold Mines):**
- Max ONE of each type
- Keep all others at deliberately low levels
- Reason: Alliance Glory and similar events award points for upgrading buildings — low-level buildings are a strategic reserve of easy event points
- Exception: one production building per type must meet TC prerequisite requirements

**Alliance assists:** Every assist received reduces building and research queue time. Always request helps and always give them.
