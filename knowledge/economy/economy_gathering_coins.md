## GATHERING

### Best Gathering Hero Lineup

**M5 gathering (peace config):** Diao Chan (lead) + Cleopatra + Darius

**Diao Chan:** Best gathering lead at any level. Signature skill grants 1.5x daily resources regardless of hero level. No other hero matches this. **Never place Diao Chan in a combat march.**

### Troop Load by Tier

| Tier | Resources per troop |
|---|---|
| T1 | 45 |
| T2 | 60 |
| T3 | 75 |
| T4 | 90 |
| T5 | 105 |
| T6 | 120 |
| T7 | 135 |

With Troop Load research lv5 (+10%): T4 carries 99 per troop, T6 carries 132, T7 carries 148.

### Resource Nodes

- Higher level nodes = more speed and greater capacity
- Rich nodes = double capacity
- Always target the highest level node available
- Node levels 1-9 confirmed; higher levels hold 5-6M resources per fill

**MEE Stage 1 strategy:** Send gathering march to highest level node the night before Stage 1 starts. Full load delivers maximum points the moment it arrives at stage start.

### Gathering Rules

- Warehouse protects a base resource amount from raiding — upgrade warehouse if consistently overflowing
- Do not use resource items unless absolutely necessary — pushing resources over protected limit makes your city a raid target
- Safe Resource items (e.g. Wood x10K Safe) can be stored pre-looted during war periods

### 2. Alliance Coins

**Earned by:** 20 donations/day + 20 assists/day + alliance events.

**Important:** Alliance Coins in the Treasury are your personal coins — not shared alliance funds.

**Priority spend (weekly reset):**

| Priority | Item |
|---|---|
| 1 | Skill Points |
| 2 | Building speedups |
| 3 | Research speedups |
| 4 | Resource Chests (balance shortfalls) |
| 5 | Recruitment Tokens |

### 3. Tactic Coins (Island Tactics)

**Earned by:** Passive production based on Island Tactics stage progression (900 stages total).

**Collection cap: 12 hours** — coins stop accumulating. Collect twice daily minimum (~10-12h apart).

**Priority spend (weekly reset):**

| Priority | Item |
|---|---|
| 1 | Iron Meteorite (ring T1/T2 upgrades) |
| 2 | Forging Tools (gear levelling) |
| 3 | Magma Crystals (gear star upgrades) |
| 4 | Gear blueprints |
| 5 | Skill Points |
| 6 | Forging speedups |

**Non-negotiable rule:** Buy every gear material item in the Island Tactics store every weekly reset. Best free source of gear crafting materials.

## DAILY & WEEKLY COIN ROUTINE

### Daily (every day)

| Task | Currency |
|---|---|
| Collect Island Tactics coins ×2 (~10-12h apart) | Tactic Coins |
| 20 alliance donations | Alliance Coins |
| 20 alliance assists | Alliance Coins |
| Defeat tribes | Merits |
| Spend Merits store | Merits (daily reset) |
| Legendary Advent free spins (8/day) | EC savings |

**Daily quest 200-point path:**
- Legendary Arrival recruitment: 40 pts
- Gather 50k resources: 30 pts

- Train 1,000 units: 20 pts
